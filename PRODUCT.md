# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
Residents and community members exploring neighbourhood terrain, rainfall and river/basin context in India. Secondary audience: college evaluators and technical readers inspecting evidence.

## Operating context
Two-day local project demonstration using Docker and an internet connection. Existing React, FastAPI, worker and PostGIS architecture. Later expansion to other countries is possible; no new trained model is required for this release.

## Capabilities and constraints
Assess a location, compare two assessments, and print a report. Coverage is limited to imported real datasets. No live waterlogging, flood probability, property safety, or official warning claims. Research models remain isolated.

## Brand commitments
Calm civic-service interface, plain-language results, expandable advanced details. Preserve honest unavailable states.
