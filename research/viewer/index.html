How the models work together

The rainfall LSTM and gradient baseline classify the whole historical chip. Extra Trees and U-Net independently map observed-water pixels. All thresholds and checkpoints come from validation events; the displayed event remained held out during fitting. The reference may include permanent rivers or lakes.
